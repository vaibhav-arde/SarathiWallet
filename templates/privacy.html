Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Sarathi Wallet, we collect information that you provide directly to us. This includes your account information (email address and password), expense records, categories, notes, and any other data you input while using the service. We do not collect sensitive financial information such as credit card numbers or bank account details.

We use the data you provide solely for the purpose of providing and improving the Sarathi Wallet service. Specifically, we use your information to maintain your expense records, display spending summaries and analytics, personalize your experience, and communicate with you about service updates. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers with industry-standard encryption and security measures. Our service may contain links to third-party websites or services that are not operated by us. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or privacy practices of any third-party sites or services.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze usage patterns, and improve our service. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may limit your ability to use certain features of the service.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings. You may also request a copy of your data or request that we delete your account and all associated data. Please note that we may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

Sarathi Wallet is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Continued use of the service after any modifications indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us. We are committed to resolving any privacy concerns you may have.
